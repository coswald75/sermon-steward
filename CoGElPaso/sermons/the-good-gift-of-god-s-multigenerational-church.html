The Good Gift of God's Multigenerational Church

Psalm 78:1-8 Pastor Jonathan Vogan
Thesis God's church is a multigenerational good gift where every generation has the duty to pass down the truth of the gospel, trust in its transforming power, tell the stories of God's faithfulness and mercy, and practice intentional disciple-making across age groups.

Theological claims· 9
  1. The central truth worth passing down is the gospel—God's faithfulness to redeem through the life, death, and resurrection of Christ—along with all that pertains to salvation and holy living. unit #5
  2. The church is described throughout the New Testament as a multigenerational expression of the kingdom of God, which will culminate in eternity when all generations from all nations worship the risen King together. unit #6
  3. Under the new covenant, passing down truth means both proclaiming salvation by grace alone through faith in Christ and calling people to holiness as those changed by grace, not as legalistic pietism. unit #9
  4. Jesus calls us to repent and be born again as new creations in Christ—the old has passed, the new has come. unit #17
  5. The truth we pass down is a truth of transformation—God saves sinners and enables them to live as redeemed people. unit #18
  6. Multigenerational churches must tell the whole truth—both stories of faithfulness and rebellion—so that coming generations can see the depth of God's mercy, learn to trust him, and avoid repeating past mistakes. unit #20
  7. God's design for the church is a multigenerational faith family where generations work together over decades to build something beautiful, not a one-second baton pass. unit #26
  8. God pours out his Spirit on all generations—sons, daughters, old men, young men—and the Holy Spirit is actively building and equipping the church across generations, fulfilling Jesus' promise to build his church. unit #39
  9. God empowers the church to do what he has called it to do. unit #40
