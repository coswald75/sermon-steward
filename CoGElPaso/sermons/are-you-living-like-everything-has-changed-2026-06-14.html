Are You Living Like Everything Has Changed?

Titus 3:1-8 June 14, 2026 Pastor Ricky Alcantar
Thesis Because God has definitively changed everything through the gospel of Jesus Christ, Christians must stop living like slaves who have been freed but continue wearing their chains, and instead live in the reality of their new identity as washed, renewed heirs of eternal life.

18 · Applies the house-buying illustration directly: Christians suffer from the same forgetfulness—anxious about the future despite holding the key to eternal life

Friends, for too many of us, that is the Christian life, isn't it? We are anxious thinking, 'Where is life going? What's going to happen next? Where is God taking me? Will He still be there? Not saying that those things are not important, not saying that those things don't matter, but friends, we have the key to heaven in our pocket. And friends, in the same way in the Christian life, we work all the harder now having been made free, not so that we can become free, but because we are free. That's what makes sense of all of Titus. Father, Awaken Us to Freedom Already Won

Father, we come before you in wonder at the gospel you have preached to us through your Son. You are a God of goodness and loving-kindness—not distant, not angry, not waiting for us to prove ourselves worthy of your love. In Christ, you have interrupted our slavery to sin and set us free. We confess that we live as though your rescue never happened. We wear the chains of shame long after you have broken them. We labor to earn a love that was given freely. We hide our failures instead of running to you, as though we could clean ourselves up before we dared approach your throne. We live small when you have made us heirs of eternal life.

But here is the good news: you have not left us to our old ways. Through the washing of regeneration and the renewal of the Holy Spirit, you have made us new—not by works we have done, but by your grace alone (Titus 3:5-7). Everything has changed. The penalty is paid. The separation is bridged. The dominion of darkness has no claim on us. We are washed. We are renewed. We are yours.

Father, awaken us this week to the reality of what you have already accomplished. Grant us the courage to stop performing and start receiving. When we fail, give us the grace to run toward you rather than away from you. Teach us to live in the freedom we already possess—not with arrogance, but with profound humility and profound safety, knowing that your love does not depend on our performance (Titus 3:3-8). Help us to believe that everything has changed, and to walk in that truth with joy and lightness, as those who have been rescued from death itself.

We commit ourselves to you this week, not to earn your love, but because we are already loved. To your name be the glory forever. Amen.

5-day reading plan

This week we meditate on what it means to live as people for whom everything has changed through Christ's salvation—moving from the chains of sin to the freedom of heirs.

Monday Matthew 27:51

When the veil tore at Christ's death, it was not a small adjustment to religious practice. It was the heavens declaring that the entire structure of separation between God and humanity had been demolished. We are no longer slaves kept at a distance; we are children invited into the holy place. Live today knowing that the barrier has been permanently removed.

Tuesday Titus 3:3-4

Paul doesn't soften the diagnosis: we were foolish, disobedient, enslaved to passions and envy. We deserved nothing. Yet into that darkness stepped God's kindness. Not because we improved ourselves or earned a second chance, but because God is good. This means your acceptance rests on his nature, not your works—a truth that both humbles pride and anchors safety.

Wednesday Titus 3:5-6

We did not clean ourselves up before God accepted us. The Holy Spirit washed us while we were still unclean. Regeneration was not our project but his work, poured out lavishly on us according to his mercy alone. This week, notice where you are still trying to earn or perfect your standing with God. Lay that down. You are already washed.

Thursday Titus 3:7

An heir does not labor to deserve the inheritance; it is secured by the will of another. Through Christ, we are justified by grace and constituted as heirs of eternal life. That inheritance is not conditional on your performance next week or next month. It is yours because you are his. Walk today with the lightness of one whose future is already secured.

Friday Titus 3:8

Paul anchors the practical call—'to devote themselves to good works'—in the foundation of what God has already done. We work not to earn salvation but because we are already saved. This week, when you stumble, do you hide and self-improve, or do you run to God with your failure in hand? The direction you turn reveals whether you truly believe the gospel has changed everything.
