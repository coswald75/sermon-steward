God's Gift of Friendship

John 15:12-17 Pastor Chuck Mosely
Thesis God created the human longing for friendship to draw us into intimacy with Himself, then places us in the local church to be formed through fellowship, and finally sends us on mission together — friendship serving as the relational vehicle for God's saving, sanctifying, and sending purposes.
