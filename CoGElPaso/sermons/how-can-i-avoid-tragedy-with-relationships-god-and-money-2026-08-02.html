How Can I Avoid Tragedy with Relationships, God, and Money?

Ecclesiastes 4-6 August 2, 2026 Pastor Ricky Alcantar
Thesis The only path to a happy life is life oriented vertically toward God in appropriate fear and horizontally outward toward neighbor, rather than bent inward on itself in envy, isolation, religious pretense, or material pursuit.

Theological claims· 9
  1. All paths to unhappiness share a common root—life bent inward on itself—while the single path to happiness is life oriented vertically toward God in appropriate fear and reverence. unit #2
  2. Nearly always, isolation over time is a choice rather than an accident, resulting from warping back in on yourself rather than reaching outward toward others. unit #8
  3. The only path to happiness in relationships is Godward and outward—loving God first, then loving neighbor in light of that love—rather than the self-focused path our culture assumes will make us happy. unit #12
  4. We would be foolish to discard holy reverence for God along with the fulfilled Old Testament rituals, because cavalier religion with closed ears and casual steps is a path to profound unhappiness. unit #15
  5. The path to a happy Christian life is moving away from self-focused religious practice and living an upward and outward Christian life grounded in whole-hearted love for God. unit #21
  6. A happy Christian life results from being so God-focused that you forget yourself—a paradox where you find your needs met precisely by not making yourself the primary focus. unit #22
  7. Wealth itself is not evil, but it brings a host of practical problems including fear of loss and sleepless anxiety that the simpler laborer does not face. unit #26
  8. Jesus offers freedom from the cage of money and experience-chasing not by stopping our fun but by redirecting us to find our life in God, which alone truly satisfies. unit #32
  9. Jesus does what the preacher of Ecclesiastes cannot—He trades His wise life for our foolish one, offering His righteousness in exchange for all our warped, inward-bent failures. unit #34
