Theological claims· 5
  1. The desire for friendship is a universal human longing and a gift of common grace from a relational God. unit #3
  2. God himself is a God of relationship, existing eternally as three persons in one God. unit #4
  3. Relationship is a common grace gift from God, rooted in his own character and extended to all humanity. unit #7
  4. God creates the human longing for intimacy not merely to connect us with other people but ultimately to draw us into relationship with himself. unit #10
  5. Spiritual intimacy with God consists of the salvific work of regeneration, forgiveness, adoption, and indwelling—all of which establish friendship between God and the believer. unit #12
