Enjoy Your Frustrating Life

Ecclesiastes 2:18-26 August 13, 2023 Pastor Ricky Alcantar
Thesis Because all human achievement and experience are ultimately vapor, we are freed to find joy in our daily work and ordinary pleasures when we root our identity not in what we produce or consume but in Christ, doing all things as worship to a God who sees and receives every moment.

Theological claims· 4
  1. Ecclesiastes holds together two seemingly contradictory truths—that all is vanity and that we are to enjoy life—and both are essential to the book's message. unit #3
  2. The fundamental problem is treating work or possessions as the source of what only God can provide: hope, peace, security, fulfillment, and satisfaction. unit #8
  3. Work that seems obscure or repetitive—stocking shelves, organizing files—has an innate goodness and joy that no one can take away because it is part of the craft God has given. unit #13
  4. God is a generous giver whose daily gifts and pleasures are already present in your ordinary life today, but you miss them while chasing future experiences. unit #18
