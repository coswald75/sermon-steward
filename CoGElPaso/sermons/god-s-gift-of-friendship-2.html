Theological claims· 5
  1. Friendship is a common grace gift from God given to all human beings, reflecting a universal deep desire for relationship and intimacy. unit #3
  2. Intimate relationship began in the heart of God and is His common grace gift to all humanity, something we know intuitively. unit #7
  3. God placed the longing for intimacy in human hearts primarily to draw us to Himself, because the greatest intimate relationship is with Him. unit #11
  4. God makes friendship possible because He loves us, and those who have not yet received Christ are here today because God is reaching out to them in friendship through His Son. unit #23
  5. Believers are yoked together shoulder to shoulder facing outward on mission to the lost, and while we should have non-Christian friends in our lives, our closest intimate friendships should be with fellow believers in the church. unit #49
