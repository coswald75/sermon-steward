Theological claims· 7
  1. The instinctive response of a believer to threats against their faith is prayer, which should be as natural and vital as breathing. unit #5
  2. The church trusts God's sovereignty in suffering because they understand that the pattern of nations raging against God continues from Psalm 2 through the cross to Revelation—and that Jesus has already won the war through his death and resurrection. unit #11
  3. Because the nations rage against Jesus himself, Jesus's people will face the same opposition and therefore need courageous prayer. unit #14
  4. Prayer is vital and natural for Christians because it expresses our desperate need for daily relationship with God who is our Father, shepherd, and lover of our souls. unit #21
  5. God answered the early church's prayer for boldness by giving them a fresh filling of the Holy Spirit. unit #24
  6. Though indwelt by the Spirit at conversion, believers are not always controlled by the Spirit, so we must continually repent of sin and pray for fresh filling to live the abundant life Jesus promised. unit #27
  7. According to Jonathan Edwards's observation of revival history, God ordains that extraordinary prayer precedes His extraordinary works, making prayer a primary means of advancing Christ's kingdom. unit #30
