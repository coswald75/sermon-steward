All of it becomes precious to him because God's protection is precious to him.

28 · Extends the house imagery through a series of domestic metaphors emphasizing the security, provision, and perfection of dwelling in God's house

Think about God's house. It has a secure foundation. Its walls are not solid. You don't have to wonder, are things going to fall apart here? The shingles on the roof are unaffected, unlike the many shingles of our homes when they encounter the hurricane winds of El Paso. In God's house, the pantry is always stocked. The beds are the perfect temperature. Not too hot, not too cold. There is no place on earth where we could be happier, not even Disneyland. And I don't know about you guys, there are just Sundays I have here where I'm like, I don't want this to end. I just want to keep being with God's people, worshiping our God together.

29 · Expounds Psalm 84:11's sun imagery by cataloging the sun's attributes through a conversation with the preacher's five-year-old son: it casts out darkness, enables sight, provides warmth, is consistent, and is the source of life

So we got this shield. We got this house. The third image, the image of the sun, verse eleven. For the Lord God is a sun and shield. The Lord bestows favor and honor. No good thing does he withhold from those who walk uprightly. What do we know about the sun? Well, I turned to Braxton, my five year old, to help me with this. I said, braxton, what do you know about the sun? And he says, well, the sun casts out darkness with its light. I said, good. He says, the sun helps us to see in the day. I say, good. He's like, it gives us warmth. And he's like, sometimes it's a little too warm here. I'm like, that's okay, but it brings us comfort. What else about the sun? He's like, it's just there. I was like, yeah, it's consistent. It's reliable, right? Sure, it's consistent. We don't have to wonder, is this going to rise today? Is it going to set today. Most importantly, we know that the sun is the source of life.

30 · Synthesizes the sun imagery into a theological claim about God as the source of life and true sight, supported by C

So what this psalmist is saying is God is the source of life who enables true and real sight. That's what God does. Cs Lewis famously says, I believe in Christianity as I believe that the sun has risen not only because I see it, but because by it I see everything else, because we find everlasting security in God's shelter and protection and we have the delight and joy of basking in his light as our guiding son. Not only that, but the master of the house is so, so generous toward his people. He holds nothing back from his people.

31 · Pivots from exposition of Psalm 84's original context to its New Testament fulfillment by reminding the congregation what the psalm originally celebrated: God's deliverance and provision in the exodus and wilderness

Take a step back. Psalm 84 was written as a song to be sung to delight and inspire people to go and worship the Lord because of what the Lord has done. He saved them from Egypt, he sustained them. He provided. That was what psalm 84 was written for church.

32 · Executes the christological payoff: the shield, house, and sun imagery of Psalm 84 find their fulfillment in Christ, who absorbed God's wrath, gave believers access to God without a temple, and secured eternal joy

How much more so today should psalm 84 produce joy and delight in our hearts? When thinking about the gathering on Sunday mornings, the original singers of psalm 84 long to see, long to have what we have today on this side of the cross. Sunday is the best day of the week because week in and week out, we are reminded that we have a shield in Jesus Christ. Jesus our shield stood between us and received the blow that you and I deserved when he hung up on the cross. Jesus our shield absorbed the holy wrath of God so that those who believed would no longer have to go to a temple to worship God, but the Holy Spirit would then dwell within us. Sunday is the best day of the week because it reminds us that Christ died for the lowliest of sinners. Sunday is the best day of the week because God strengthens us through his word, our worship and one another. Even when suffering and difficulty comes, our eternal joy is secured forever that will never ever be taken from us.

33 · Extends the christological claim into eschatological hope: the same God who provided salvation will sustain believers to the end, when they will be face to face with Christ forever

I have more Sunday is the best day of the week because we are reminded that the same God who has provided salvation for his people is the same God who will sustain us to the end when we are face to face with Jesus Christ in his presence forever. One Peter one eight says, though we do not see him, we love him. Church long for that day when we will be face to face with him forever. Rely on his shield to get us there. God is faithful. He will protect us on this pilgrimage until we have arrived safely home. Sunday reminds us that we are secure, that we are freed from our sins to love God the way we were created to love him. And lastly, Sunday is a reminder that God does not withhold anything good because he gives us what our hearts long for most, what every human being was created for. He gives us himself. Amen.

34 · Returns to Psalm 84:12 and issues a diagnostic question: does Christ as sun and shield move the congregation to delight in the Sunday gathering?

Verse twelve O Lord of hosts, blessed is the one who trusts in you. Church. Does Christ our sun and shield move you to delight? Do you see why Sunday morning is much bigger than just a duty, but a delight that we get to be a part of? Blessed is the one who trusts in goddesse.

35 · Defines trust in the Lord not as passive compliance but as inexpressible joy and delight in a secure relationship with a trustworthy God

And here's the thing. When it comes to trusting the Lord, what trusting the Lord doesn't look like is this nonchalant, lackadaisical attitude. Trusting in the Lord is inexpressible joy and delight. There is no one like our God who welcomes us into a relationship with him that is secure and trustworthy.

36 · Direct evangelistic charge to the unconverted: abandon false shields and turn to Christ for protection

I just want to say, if you are not in Christ, what is the shield that you are holding up thinking will protect you? Behold the shield of God. Jesus Christ offers you his protection today. Would you repent and turn to his protection?

37 · Direct charge to believers: when church feels like duty, remember the four-fold reality of God's relationship to his people — above, before, for, and lavishing love — drawn from Psalm 84's imagery

Love now for those of us who attend church regularly, who would say, I follow Jesus, he is my savior, I am his. We are still going to have days where church is just going to feel like a duty. But may psalm 84 be our guide when we feel church is a duty rather than a delight? Let us remember these four things about psalm 84 God is above you, illuminating your highway as a sun. God is before you, protecting and securing you as a shield. And God is for you, bestowing favor and honor toward his people. And lastly, God is lavishing, he lavishes his love for us, withholding nothing good from us.
