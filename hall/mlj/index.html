Corpus Note

David Martyn Lloyd-Jones was minister at Westminster Chapel, London (1939–1968). Physician-turned-preacher. His Friday night lectures and Sunday sermons produced some of the longest expository series in Christian history: Romans (366 sermons), Ephesians (80). "Preaching is theology coming through a man who is on fire." The Doctor's sermons are logical argument conducted at emotional intensity — hence: Logic on Fire.

The Lloyd-Jones Signature

MLJ's signature is the sustained logical argument building across the full sermon length. He rarely illustrates — he argues. Each paragraph is a move in a logical chain, and the emotional heat builds as the logic tightens. His Romans series is the gold standard of verse-by-verse expository preaching. The physician's diagnostic method shapes everything: he identifies the presenting symptom, traces the root cause, then prescribes the gospel cure.
