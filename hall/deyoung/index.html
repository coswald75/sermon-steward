The DeYoung Signature

DeYoung's signature is accessible theological clarity. Of all the Hall preachers, he moves most directly from text to congregation without academic detour. His sermons are structured with visible scaffolding — the congregation always knows where they are in the argument. He proves that Reformed expository preaching can be warm, clear, and disarmingly readable without sacrificing depth.
