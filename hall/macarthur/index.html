Corpus Note

Founder of Grace to You media ministry and The Master's University and Seminary. Strict verse-by-verse exposition through complete books of Scripture. The MacArthur Signature

MacArthur's signature is relentless verse-by-verse textual immersion. No text is too long, no passage too detailed. His sermons can run 50+ minutes working through three verses. The application is implicit — he trusts that careful exposition produces transformation. Of all the Hall preachers, he is least concerned with cultural engagement and most concerned with textual completeness.
