Corpus Note

S. Lewis Johnson Jr. taught New Testament at Dallas Theological Seminary and was a founding elder of Believers Chapel in Dallas. 1,642 sermons recorded and archived at SLJInstitute.net. Trained in classical exegesis, he eventually moved from dispensationalism toward Reformed theology while maintaining a rigorous grammatical-historical method. His sermon preparation was legendary for thoroughness.

The Johnson Signature

SLJ's signature is exhaustive exegetical thoroughness. He misses nothing in the text. His sermons move through every word, every grammatical construct, every cross-reference — and yet never become pedantic because his theological synthesis is so sure. He is the Hall's premier student of the text itself — less concerned with illustration or application structure than with getting the exegetical foundation absolutely right.
