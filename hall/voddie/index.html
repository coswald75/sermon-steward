Corpus Note

Voddie Baucham Jr. is Dean of the Seminary at African Christian University in Zambia, formerly Grace Family Baptist in Spring, TX. Bold expository preacher with a distinctive emphasis on biblical manhood, family discipleship, and cultural critique from a Reformed Baptist framework. Known for engaging intersectionality, social justice debates, and the sufficiency of Scripture.

The Baucham Signature

Baucham's signature is the confrontational theological argument — he preaches as though the stakes of getting the text wrong are civilizational. His cultural engagement is direct where other Hall preachers are indirect. He names the ideologies, refutes them by name, and drives the text through them like a wedge. The combination of warm pastoral concern with hard polemical edge is his distinctive.
