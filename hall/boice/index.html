Corpus Note

James Montgomery Boice served at Tenth Presbyterian Church in Philadelphia for 32 years (1968–2000). Co-founder of the Alliance of Confessing Evangelicals and the Cambridge Declaration. Strong opponent of the seeker-sensitive movement and the "dumbing down" of evangelical worship and preaching. His multi-volume expository commentaries grew directly from his Sunday sermons: Genesis, John, Romans, Psalms, Daniel.

The Boice Signature

Boice's signature is confident Reformed exposition without apology. He preaches the confessional Reformed tradition as a fully integrated system — no theological question is too technical, no doctrinal nuance too subtle. Where others might soften a hard text, Boice lets it stand. His long 10th Presbyterian tenure means his congregation was formed by decades of consecutive expository preaching.
