Romans 12:1-2 September 1, 1994 James Boice
Thesis Christians in the modern West have lost a distinctively Christian mind and must recover it through intentional engagement with Scripture and doctrine, resisting the mindless entertainment culture that has infiltrated even evangelical churches.

Theological claims· 18
  1. Modern evangelicals have lost a distinctively Christian frame of reference by which they think differently from the secular world, making them virtually indistinguishable from non-Christians in their thought patterns and resulting behavior. unit #5
  2. Paul's command in Romans 12 to avoid conformity to the world and to be transformed by mind renewal is a direct mandate to reject secular thought patterns (secularism, humanism, relativism, materialism) and instead think as God thinks. unit #6
  3. The cultural problem has deteriorated since 1963 from loss of Christian thinking to the near-absence of deep thinking about anything at all—a genuinely mindless age. unit #7
  4. Television is the chief cause of the mindlessness characterizing modern culture, more significant than materialism, skepticism, or busyness in destroying capacity for serious thought. unit #9
  5. Television's power lies not in transmitting information but in presenting empty images onto which viewers project their own desires, making ignorance rather than knowledge the basis of cultural engagement. unit #13
  6. Television produces not merely ignorance but ignorance of ignorance—viewers believe themselves informed while actually knowing very little, having projected desires onto empty images rather than engaging actual reality. unit #14
  7. The cultural shift from typography to television represents a move from word-based communication that fosters thought to image-based communication that suppresses thought, fundamentally altering Western consciousness. unit #18
  8. Television news exists not to inform but to sell advertising by maintaining viewer attention, which requires preventing genuine human emotional response to tragedy and rapidly shifting between disconnected segments. unit #22
  9. If politics is show business (as Reagan claimed), then political success depends on appearing to have integrity and competence rather than actually possessing them, since the essence of show business is convincing pretense. unit #24
  10. Television religion loses what matters most—a sense of God's transcendent presence—because the medium can only present the preacher's image on screen, structurally displacing God with celebrity and making genuine worship impossible. unit #34
  11. Television's cultural dominance creates irresistible pressure on churches to adopt entertainment standards—short sermons, theological avoidance, emotional uplift, story-driven content—making churches 'amusing' (literally, not-thinking) rather than spiritually formative, while treating boredom complaints as validation rather than the problem. unit #37
  12. Evangelical churches suffer from 'the weightlessness of God' and 'the inconsequentiality of theology' (David Wells)—orthodox beliefs are held but make no practical difference because they are not being seriously thought about or taught. unit #39
  13. The most urgent need in the evangelical church today is the recovery of serious theological thinking among Christians—both corporately in churches and individually in believers' lives. unit #40
  14. Genuine worship requires mental engagement with Scripture to know God's true worth; mindless worship is impossible because worship is ascribing God's worth, and without knowledge of God from His Word, what churches do is worship man through self-focused songs and felt-needs messages. unit #42
  15. Entertainment-driven evangelism fails to produce genuine conversions because 'what you win them with is what you win them to'—people attracted by entertainment stay for entertainment and leave if teaching replaces it, trapping churches in perpetual entertainment mode that grows attendance without advancing the kingdom. unit #43
  16. Jesus prioritized preaching over healing because healed people can still die and go to hell, but those who hear, understand, repent, and believe the gospel go to heaven—which is why entertainment-driven church growth that bypasses serious gospel proclamation is not kingdom work. unit #45
  17. Evangelicals' statistical indistinguishability from secular culture in behavior demonstrates failure in sanctification caused by biblical and doctrinal ignorance—either from lack of teaching or from apathy toward what is taught. unit #46
  18. Paul's approach to sanctification is fundamentally rational: since God has irreversibly joined you to Christ and you cannot return to union with Adam, the only logical response is to 'grow up' and live consistently with your new identity rather than childishly acting like what you no longer are. unit #49

Daily readings · Monday–Friday

5-day reading plan

This week traces the path from our loss of Christian thinking (Monday) through the cultural forces that have destroyed our capacity for serious thought (Tuesday–Thursday) to the recovery Paul calls us toward through intentional mind renewal (Friday).

Monday Romans 5

Romans 5 establishes what we should think: that we have peace with God through Christ, that we rejoice in suffering because it produces endurance and character, that God's love is poured into our hearts. The tragedy of our mindlessness is that we possess these truths but do not think upon them—we live as though we are still at enmity with God, still enslaved to fear, still pursuing the world's hollow promises. Our thought patterns betray our actual beliefs.

Tuesday Mark 1:21-39

Notice how Jesus deliberately withdraws from the crowds healed in Capernaum to preach in other towns—the healing didn't produce enduring faith, but the proclamation of the gospel does. This exposes the rot in our entertainment-driven churches: we offer the equivalent of healing without gospel, attracting people with felt-needs uplift and emotional experience while starving them of the Word that actually transforms. We have become craftsmen of temporary satisfaction rather than architects of eternal conversion.

Wednesday John 4:24

Genuine worship in spirit and truth demands we know whom we worship—not a vague feeling of transcendence, but actual knowledge of God's character, His holiness, His redemptive acts, His majesty. When our churches cultivate emotional atmosphere without doctrinal depth, we are manufacturing a counterfeit: we are worshiping our own sentiments, our felt needs, our therapeutic comfort. We ascribe worth to man, not God. The stakes could not be higher.

Thursday Mark 1:14

Jesus' first public proclamation is 'the time is fulfilled, and the kingdom of God has come near; repent, and believe in the good news.' Repentance includes a revolution of the mind—turning from one frame of reference (the kingdom of this world) to another (the kingdom of God). To be formed by Scripture and doctrine is not optional enrichment; it is obedience to the fundamental call to submit our thinking to Christ's lordship. We cannot neutrally absorb secular philosophy and simultaneously serve the gospel.

Friday Romans 6:11

Paul's logic is irreducibly rational: 'consider yourselves dead to sin and alive to God in Christ Jesus.' We are unified with Christ; we cannot return to Adam's dominion; therefore we must grow up and act like what we actually are. Recovery begins when we stop accepting the cultural premise that thinking deeply is optional, turn off the screens that hollow out our minds, and seriously engage Scripture until our thoughts align with God's thoughts. This is not burden but the grateful, logical response to grace.
