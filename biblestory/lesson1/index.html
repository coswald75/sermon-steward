  • God commanded Adam and Eve (and Noah) to “multiply and fill the earth” (1:28 and 9:7). The people in Gen 11 refused to be “dispersed over the face of the whole earth” (11:4).
  • God created human beings to be his image-bearers (1:27). The people in Gen 11 rebelled against God by deciding to make a name for themselves rather than for God (11:4).
  • Both of these acts of rebellion undercut God’s design to establish a global kingdom through humans who would reign as his image-bearers.
In Gen 1–11, God established his kingdom through humans. When they rejected him as King, God announced judgment and hope.
