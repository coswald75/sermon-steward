Bible Seminar 2

Knowing the Bible’s Story

Nine lessons tracing the Bible’s single storyline, from creation to new creation.

For his glory, God is reconciling the world to himself under King Jesus.

The Bible is not a loose anthology — it tells one story. Each lesson below walks through a major section of Scripture, reads the key passages together, and ends with a reflection on how that part of the story prepared for Jesus. Tap any blank as you go: your answers save automatically on your device.
