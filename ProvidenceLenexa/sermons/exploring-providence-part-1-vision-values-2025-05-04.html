This week we meditate on Providence Community Church's vision and identity: a Reformed church anchored in Scripture, centered on Christ's gospel, empowered by the Spirit, led by faithful elders, and commissioned to plant churches and pursue unity in the body of Christ.

Monday 2 Timothy 3:16-17

Paul affirms that all Scripture is God-breathed and profitable for teaching, rebuking, correcting, and training in righteousness. As we embrace this vision of truth and beauty, we are anchored not in cultural trends or human opinion, but in the living authority of God's Word—the same Word that equipped the saints in every generation and equips us today for the whole counsel of God.

Tuesday Romans 11:36

"From him and through him and to him are all things." This doxology captures the Reformed conviction that undergirds Providence: God's sovereignty is not a cold doctrine but a ground for worship. All our plans, all our resources, all our church-planting dreams flow from his eternal purpose and return to his glory, filling us with gratitude and humility as we labor.

Wednesday Luke 24:44-47

The risen Jesus opens the disciples' minds to see that the Law, the Prophets, and the Psalms all speak of him: his suffering, his resurrection, and the proclamation of repentance and forgiveness in his name to all nations. This is the theological north star for Providence: every passage we study, every sermon we hear, every doctrine we confess points to Christ as the fulfillment of God's redemptive purpose and the hope of our witness.

Thursday 1 Corinthians 12:4-13

Paul teaches that one Spirit gives different gifts—wisdom, knowledge, faith, healing, prophecy, and tongues—to each believer as he wills, so that the body of Christ functions in vibrant interdependence. We affirm with joy that the Spirit is not a historical force but alive and active among us, filling believers with power for Christlikeness and for supernatural witness, cementing our unity as we steward these gifts in love.

Friday Matthew 28:19-20

The Great Commission is not a suggestion but a binding mandate from the risen Christ himself: go, make disciples, teach them all that Christ commanded. As Providence, we embrace this commission with joy, desiring to plant churches as God provides resources and to grow together in faith and witness—not for our own glory, but so that all nations might hear of Christ's redemptive work and bow before him.

Pray together this week

Truth and Beauty in Community

Father, we come before you with wonder at your sovereign grace that has gathered us as Providence Community Church. We marvel at your commitment to reveal yourself through Scripture, that authoritative Word by which you speak your truth into our lives, and we thank you for the beauty, goodness, and flourishing you call us to pursue together for your glory. We confess that we often default to isolated living, content with partial obedience, and slow to embrace the radical interdependence and complementary partnerships you have designed for us. We admit our tendency to fragment around personalities or preferences rather than rally around the centrality of Christ and the gospel that binds us together.

Yet the gospel humbles and fills us with gratitude: Christ, through his substitutionary work, has broken down every barrier between us and God and between one another (Ephesians 2:14–16). In his resurrection, he has ascended as our Head over the church, and he continues to pour out his Spirit upon us, animating us with power not only for personal sanctification but for supernatural witness and corporate witness to all nations. He has given us the gift of elder plurality to shepherd us in truth, and he has commissioned us to make disciples by planting churches that embody this vision of truth and beauty in community.

We ask that you would deepen our conviction that the gospel is the pinnacle of your redemptive acts and the center of all Scripture. Grant us grace to listen to your Word with humble, submissive hearts, and empower us by your Spirit to live out the distinct and complementary roles you have given to men and women, that we might display your design with joy and faith. Make us a people increasingly interdependent with one another and with our sisters and brothers in Sovereign Grace Churches regionally and globally, that the unity for which Jesus prayed might find concrete expression in our fellowship and shared mission. To the Head of the church, who reigns over all creation and all history toward the redemption of sinners, be all glory, honor, and dominion forever.
