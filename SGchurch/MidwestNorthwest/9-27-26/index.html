How these numbers are made

  • Sources. Each church's own public sermon feed, website, or YouTube livestream for the Sunday shown. One sermon per church. No archives were imported. Providence's sermon was already on Sermon Steward.
  • Transcripts are machine transcriptions (AssemblyAI). For full-service livestreams, only the sermon (first word to closing prayer) was kept. Podcast files are used as published and may include a short reading or closing line.
  • Segments and tags come from Sermon Steward's breakdown model, which tags each segment by function (exposition, application, illustration…), doctrinal loci, Scripture citations, quotations, and biblical-theological moves. These are model readings, not hand counts.
  • Derived: word shares, application share, and words per minute are simple arithmetic on the transcript and tags.
  • Sermon length for livestreams is measured from the sermon's start and end timestamps. For podcast files it is the length of the published audio.
