Try Sermon Steward
  • As spoken. Each quotation is the text from the sermon transcript, shown with the author the preacher named. Quotes longer than 60 words are cut with “…” and marked “excerpt”. Quotes under 12 words are left out.
  • Kind of source. A simple rule sorts quotes into “Classic & pastoral” (historic pastors and well-known teachers, or books read aloud) and “Commentary & book reading” (the preacher introduced a commentary, study Bible, or article, or the author is a commentator). It is a rough guide, not a judgment.
  • Topics. Topics are matched automatically from keywords in the quote (up to three each). Some quotes have no topic, and some topics will catch quotes that only touch them.
  • Names. Where the transcript garbled an author's name and it could not be confirmed, the quote appears under “Other / unverified” with no name shown. Organization statistics, Scripture, and anonymous sources are left out.
