They were supposed to feel as if they were in Rome in miniature. And citizenship in Rome was not given out just because you were born or lived somewhere. It had to be acquired. It required a great deal of service, and it was weighty for those who had it and those who wanted it. Citizenship was important, and though they had conquered and ruled almost all of the known world, there were far less that had actual citizenship in Rome.
